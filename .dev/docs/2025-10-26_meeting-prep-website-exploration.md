## let's explore: what IS a website?

### a website can be many things..

**a digital home**
- where people come to understand who you are
- first impression for most people
- always open, never sleeps

**a tool**
- collects email addresses
- shares information
- sells courses or products
- processes donations
- hosts communities

**a story**
- tells your mission in a way that moves people
- shows your personality and values
- creates emotional connection

**a garden**
- grows and changes over time
- needs tending and care
- some parts bloom, some need pruning
- attracts different visitors in different seasons

### questions to explore together

**about purpose:**
- what do we want people to *feel* when they visit?
- what do we want them to *do* after visiting?
- who are we speaking to? (curious omnivores? new vegans? experienced activists?)
- what makes someone say "yes, THIS is for me"?

**about joy:**
- how do we make the experience delightful?
- what would make someone smile while browsing?
- can visiting the site feel like a warm hug rather than a lecture?
- where can we add surprise and delight?

**about authenticity:**
- what's the real vegan hearts voice? (serious? playful? warm? bold?)
- how do we avoid sounding like every other vegan org?
- what stories do we actually want to tell?
- what are we genuinely building vs what sounds good?

**about compassion:**
- how do we welcome people wherever they are in their journey?
- can the site feel safe for asking "dumb questions"?
- how do we show compassion for animals without overwhelming humans?
- where's the line between inspiring action and creating guilt?
