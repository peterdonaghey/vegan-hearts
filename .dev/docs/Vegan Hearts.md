Vegan Hearts.md

I've met a girl when I was on Rainbow just now who is a part of a startup NGO called Vegan Hearts, an organization who wants to get funding and spend effort initiating projects, events, information, websites for veganism, for the animals and for the good of the planet. So yeah, I'm pretty excited about this. I have this job now for five years coding for Point Topic, selling datasets to broadband companies and I've been dreaming of how I can use these skills for something I really believe in and this really feels like a magical opportunity. Seems like they're gonna need websites and maybe other things set up that I can help a lot with. They don't have the funding yet so they're not paying at this moment but everyone else is collecting their hours and then they should get paid afterwards. Then there is funding. Financially I'm pretty safe so I feel really good to put my efforts in here without needing to see money back right away. It's Friday 24th of October now and today at one o'clock I'm gonna have a meeting with Mirelle first and then with the rest of the VeganHearts team. So yeah, I'm thinking just to be prepared as I can for this meeting also to maybe demonstrate in which ways I can help.

I'm thinking I can already check out which domain names are available, good domain names for vegan hearts.

I'm even imagining to set up the boilerplate for a website already. Just get the bones in place and get everything even deployed in production and just have a blank site or just a title page. I could even do that pretty fast.

But I guess most importantly I should be prepared for the meeting to have the right questions to understand exactly where we are, where we're going, what everyone else is envisioning, what we imagine in terms of website or like things that I can do to help as a coder.
