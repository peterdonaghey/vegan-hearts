## 3. Strategic Questions for the Meeting

**Organizational Clarity:**

1. Who are the core leadership team members and what are their roles?
2. What's the timeline for securing funding? Any applications submitted?
3. Is there a registered nonprofit entity yet, or in progress?

**Vision & Priorities:**

4. Of all the features listed in the document, which 3 are MOST critical for year one?
5. Who is the primary target audience? (new vegans, activists, entrepreneurs, all?)
6. What makes VeganHearts different from existing organizations like The Vegan Society or Vegan Outreach?

**Technical & Practical:**

7. Does anyone else on the team have technical skills (design, content, marketing)?
8. What's the plan for content creation? (who writes, films, designs?)
9. Are there existing partnerships or collaborations in place?
10. What's the volunteer management structure? How will hours be tracked?

**Immediate Next Steps:**

11. Is there a branding package (logo, colors, fonts) or does that need creating?
12. What can realistically be launched in the next 3-6 months?
