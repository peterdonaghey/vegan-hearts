We are so excited to share something close to our hearts with you.

### A Journey Begins

In January 2026, three friends — Mirella, Eveliina, and Santeri — landed in Mumbai with open hearts and a simple mission: to connect with animals, share healing songs, and spread compassion wherever we go.

Santeri is our filmmaker and a student at the media school. Mirella and Eveliina are passionate animal lovers ready to help those in need. Together, we're creating **"Vegan Hearts in India"** — an educational, good-natured documentary about animals and world peace.

### Our Mission

Our goal is to sing and play healing songs for animals, bring them food, and rescue those in urgent need. 

We believe that humanity and the animal kingdom are all from the same light of Love. From this light our hearts shine, and it feels meaningful to help others open their vegan hearts as well.

### Connecting Hearts Worldwide

This documentary is more than just a film — it's an invitation.

We want to connect people from all over the world to participate in a world peace meditation, chant our mantra together, and let joy move within us like a dance of the soul.

### The Journey So Far

We've already begun networking with incredible souls in India:
- Connected with a filmmaker who made a documentary about India's dairy industry — we're meeting soon in Mumbai to learn from his experience
- Met a Bollywood audio engineer who has opened doors for us to visit sound studios
- Exploring the beautiful landscapes around Lonavala and discovering sacred connections with nature

Every encounter feels like a gift. Every animal we meet reminds us why this work matters.

### What's Next

We'll be in India for a month, filming, connecting, and spreading seeds of kindness and goodwill. 

The documentary will be published later in 2026, and if this journey goes well, we plan to continue this path in other countries as well.

### Support the Journey

Creating a documentary with heart takes resources. We're raising funds to make this dream a reality — to bring professional filming equipment, provide food and care for animals we encounter, and share this message of compassion with the world.

If you feel called to support this project, you can contribute here:
**[Support Vegan Hearts in India Documentary](https://gofund.me/8724c869d)**

We sincerely thank you for every single contribution, for every kind word, for every heart that opens. Together, we're planting seeds that will grow into forests of compassion.

### Stay Connected

We'll be sharing updates, photos, and behind-the-scenes moments throughout our journey. Follow along as we discover the beautiful connection between humans and animals, and remember the light we all share.

Much Love from India 💚

— Eveliina & Mirella
