- **vibe:** festival energy, bouncy, exploratory
- **url:** vegan-hearts-git-theme-playful-peterdonaghey.vercel.app

### 2. elegant magazine (`theme/elegant`)

- **layout:** editorial article format with breathing room

- **vibe:** kinfolk magazine, calm, sophisticated
- **url:** vegan-hearts-git-theme-elegant-peterdonaghey.vercel.app

### 3. bold modern (`theme/modern`)
- **structure:** split-screen hero + asymmetric grid
- **layout:** full-width color blocks, varied section sizes
- **fonts:** dm sans (clean, confident)
- **vibe:** tech with heart, dynamic, contemporary
- **url:** vegan-hearts-git-theme-modern-peterdonaghey.vercel.app
